The challenge

Therapists couldn’t see what happened between visits.

Most physical therapy recovery happens at home, in exercise programs the therapist never sees. RTM Kinetics needed a way to monitor that work remotely.

Without it, poor form went uncorrected, reps were skipped or rushed, and range of motion was a guess. Therapists only found out at the next appointment, often after progress had stalled.

Patient self-reports were the only signal, and they weren’t reliable enough to adjust a care plan or show that a program was working.

The team needed objective movement data captured at home, with no special hardware, and feedback that reached the patient while they were still exercising.

The solution

Computer vision that watches, measures and coaches in real time.

CodeSquad built a real-time tracking and motion analysis system that turns any camera into a remote monitoring tool.

  1. Detect

    AI pose estimation finds the patient’s body keypoints frame by frame and draws a live skeleton over the video.

  2. Measure

    Joint angles at the shoulder, elbow, hip and knee are calculated and compared with the target range for each exercise.

  3. Score

    Every movement is counted as a rep and scored for form, with angle deviations tracked over time.

  4. Coach

    When the patient drifts out of range, AI feedback explains the fix in plain language, using front and side views.

  5. Sync

    Each session is recorded and synced to the therapist’s dashboard, replacing self-reports with real movement data.

Conclusion

RTM Kinetics turned home exercise from a blind spot into measured, coached sessions. Patients get real-time form correction from a standard camera, and therapists get objective movement data without being in the room.

Because the tracking data flows into the same system that manages the patient relationship, the team has a complete view of every patient, from first contact through measurable recovery.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do patients need special hardware?

No. The body tracking runs on a standard camera, such as a laptop webcam or phone, with no wearables or sensors.

Can therapists see sessions remotely?

Yes. Each session’s reps, score, joint-angle data and feedback are synced to the therapist dashboard for remote review.

Will this replace our existing platform?

No. It wraps around your existing system and connects tracking data to the patient record you already use.
