The challenge

Product-of-the-month, automated.

Product-of-the-month campaigns required repetitive image work, data enrichment, analysis, and coordination between sales and marketing systems.

Manual image production

Each campaign required repeated creative preparation and formatting.

Disconnected customer data

Campaign inputs and Salesforce context lived in separate workflows.

Limited analysis

Teams spent time assembling information before they could act on it.

The solution

A connected system designed around the way the team actually works.

We built an automated image and campaign pipeline that enriches product data, prepares creative assets, and connects the result to Salesforce and email workflows.
