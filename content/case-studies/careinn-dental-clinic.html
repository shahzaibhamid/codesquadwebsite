Brief

A new clinic with no patient pipeline.

CareInn Dental Clinic launched with no existing patients, no lead history, and no search presence. Every new patient had to come from somewhere: Google, the website, phone calls, or referrals.

A new clinic can’t afford slow replies or dropped leads, because there is no returning patient base to fall back on. CareInn needed demand and a system to handle it in place before the doors opened. We built both:

  • Google Ads campaigns. Search and Performance Max campaigns targeting local patients looking for dental care.
  • SEO groundwork. A site built to rank for local dental searches, so organic visibility grows alongside paid.
  • 60-second lead response. Every enquiry is captured and answered automatically, day or night.
  • 14-day automated follow-up and one live dashboard showing every lead and its status.

Stack: Website + Google Ads + Make.com + Airtable + SMS + Email + custom dashboard.

Results

Steady patient demand from a standing start.

From Jun 2 to Oct 28, 2026, Google Ads turned PKR 101,161.98 in spend into a steady flow of conversions:

  • 338 conversions from 83,612 ad impressions
  • PKR 299 average cost per conversion, at an average cost per click of PKR 29.90
  • 10–12% conversion rate on every campaign: about 1 in 10 ad clicks became a conversion
  • 198 conversions at PKR 221 each from the best-performing search campaign
  • 92 conversions at a 12.03% conversion rate from Performance Max, the highest rate of any campaign

Organic search grew alongside the ads. Google Search Console, May 20 – Aug 29, 2026:

  • 31.4K search impressions and 248 clicks from Google Search
  • Average position of 11.9, with many local dental searches sitting just outside page one
  • 0.7% average click-through rate, with room to grow as rankings move onto page one

Every one of those enquiries goes through the same pipeline: captured, answered within 60 seconds, followed up, and tracked in one dashboard.
