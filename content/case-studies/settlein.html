The challenge

How SettleIn Turned Manual Document Review Into an Automated Eligibility Engine

SettleIn needed to process a high volume of applicant documents, but reading and evaluating each one by hand didn't scale.

Documents arrived in inconsistent formats, and pulling the right information out of each one took manual review before anyone could even start an eligibility assessment.

Eligibility scoring itself was subjective and slow when done manually, and applicants often waited without any sense of where their case stood.

Without a connected system, the team was spending more time processing paperwork than helping applicants move forward.

The solution

A connected system designed around the way the team actually works.

CodeSquad built SettleIn an automated document-to-decision pipeline.

CodeSquad built a system that uses OCR to extract information directly from uploaded documents, runs eligibility scoring automatically against the criteria that matter, and generates AI-assisted feedback so applicants understand where they stand. An admin dashboard gives the team oversight of every application, and a tracking system keeps each case moving through review without manual chasing.

Stack: OCR/document understanding + AI eligibility scoring + admin dashboard + application tracking.
