The challenge

How Debate Partners Turned Research Into Ready-to-Run Cases

Debate Partners needed to help teams and coaches turn raw research into competition-ready cases, but that process was almost entirely manual.

Building a case meant cross-referencing sources by hand, compiling evidence packets, and tracking citations separately from the case itself — slow work that didn't scale across multiple formats like Policy, LD, and Public Forum.

Managing debate teams and coaches sat in a different system entirely from the case-building work, and subscriptions and billing were handled separately again.

Without a connected platform, coaches were spending more time assembling paperwork than coaching.

The solution

A connected system designed around the way the team actually works.

CodeSquad built Debate Partners a connected case-generation and team-management platform. CodeSquad built a system that analyzes and cross-references source documents automatically, generates debate cases with evidence packets and citations attached, and supports multiple competitive formats out of the box. A CRM keeps teams and coaches organized, and subscription management and billing run through the same platform — turning case research, team management, and billing into one system instead of three.

Stack: document analysis/cross-referencing + AI case generation + CRM + subscription billing.
