The challenge

Manual content workflow

Content needed to perform in traditional search and AI-generated answers.

The solution

Search & AI visibility

The numbers behind the discoverability layer.

Live reporting pulls traditional search (SEO) and AI answer-engine (AEO) performance into one view. The SEO figures cover Jul 29 – Oct 26, 2026; the AEO analysis was run on Oct 25, 2026 across GPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek and Google AI Overviews.

SEO · Search clicks

51,599

Clicks from search across 422,499 impressions between Jul 29 and Oct 26, 2026.

SEO · Click-through rate

12.2%

Average CTR at an average ranking position of 5.8.

SEO · Keyword rankings

340 of 500

Tracked keywords on page one: 100 in positions 1–3 and 240 in positions 4–10.

AEO · Brand mention rate

18.8%

Share of AI answers that mention ENERGYbits, across 48 responses from 6 AI engines.

AEO · Sources tracked

487 citations

Citations spanning 234 unique domains, with the brand present in 5 of 8 tracked questions.

AEO · Strongest topic

83.3%

Brand mention rate on the gut-health and natural-detox question, the strongest of the 8 tracked.
